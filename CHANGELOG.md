## v1.0.0 (2026-10-20)

This is the baseline release, written by hand; earlier history wasn't in Conventional Commit
form. It covers:

- **eReferral:** a specialist referral bundle (ServiceRequest, Task, clinical-context
  DocumentReference), with an optional AU Patient Summary attachment.
- **Diagnostic requests:** AU eRequesting pathology and imaging bundles, with embedded Patient,
  practitioner and placer/filler Organizations, conforming to `au-ereq-bundle-04`.
- **Provider lookup:** Health Connect Provider Directory search, with offline fallback data.
- **Filler views:** a specialist referral inbox, and the airport board for task groups (business
  status `service-booked`).
- **Imaging appointment booking** (Radiology Referral IG):
  - the filler publishes availability
  - the placer searches Slots and proposes an Appointment
  - the filler confirms, declines or books directly
  - cancel and atomic reschedule
- **Directory Import:** HCPD bulk `$export` into the referral server, in standard and
  incremental modes.
- **SMART App Launch**, FHIR bundle visualisation (Mermaid), and the AI-map code search.
